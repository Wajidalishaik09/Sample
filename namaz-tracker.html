Viral Myth vs. Islamic Reality

The viral list that gives each prayer one worldly reward (beauty, money, health…) has no basis in the Qur'an or authentic Hadith. We pray to worship Allah — and Allah puts barakah in our whole life.
PrayerViral claim ✗Authentic teaching ✓
FajrBeauty / facial glowUnder Allah's protection for the day (Muslim 657). The 2 Sunnah are better than the world and all in it (Muslim 725).
DhuhrMore moneyGates of heaven open at this hour (Tirmidhi 478). 4 Sunnah before + 4 after: Allah forbids the Fire from you (Abu Dawud 1269).
AsrBetter healthMissing it is like losing your family and wealth (Bukhari 552). Fajr + Asr → Paradise (Bukhari 574).
MaghribPeaceful futureThe five prayers wipe away minor sins between them (Muslim 233). Pray it promptly.
IshaPeaceful sleepIn congregation = half the night in prayer; with Fajr in congregation = the whole night (Muslim 656).

Nafl Prayers: Complete Guide

🌙 Tahajjud — the Night Prayer
Why

"The best prayer after the obligatory prayers is the prayer at night." — Sahih Muslim 1163. In the last third of every night Allah descends to the lowest heaven and says: "Who is calling upon Me, that I may answer him? Who is asking of Me, that I may give him? Who is seeking My forgiveness, that I may forgive him?" — Sahih al-Bukhari 1145. Allah commanded it to the Prophet ﷺ: "And from part of the night, pray Tahajjud as extra for you" — Qur'an 17:79.

When to pray

Any time after Isha until Fajr begins. Best: the last third of the night, after some sleep.

Start small and stay consistent — even 2 rak'ahs once a week is better than a big plan you drop.

Before you sleep
  1. Make a firm niyyah to wake up for Tahajjud, and ask Allah to wake you.
  2. Sleep early, in wudu, on your right side. Read Ayat al-Kursi, the 3 Quls and the last 2 verses of Al-Baqarah.
  3. Set an alarm 30–60 minutes before Fajr, and keep the phone away from your bed.
  4. If you fear you won't wake, pray Witr after Isha (Sahih Muslim 755). Then at night pray in 2s without repeating Witr.
When you wake up
  1. Say the waking dua: Alhamdu lillahil-ladhi ahyana ba'da ma amatana wa ilayhin-nushur (below).
  2. Say the night-waking dhikr from Sahih al-Bukhari 1154 (below) — the Prophet ﷺ said that whoever says it and then makes dua is answered, and if he makes wudu and prays, his prayer is accepted.
  3. The Prophet ﷺ would look at the sky and recite the last 10 verses of Surah Aal-Imran (3:190–200) — Sahih al-Bukhari 4569.
  4. Use miswak, make wudu, and pray where you are alone and calm.
What to recite — rak'ah by rak'ah
  1. Rak'ah 1–2 (light opening): the Prophet ﷺ began night prayer with two short rak'ahs (Sahih Muslim 768). Fatihah + Al-Ikhlas, Fatihah + Al-Kawthar or any short surah.
  2. Rak'ah 3–8 (longer, in pairs): recite what you know, slowly and with meaning. Good choices:
    • Ayat al-Kursi (2:255) and last 2 verses of Al-Baqarah (2:285–286)
    • Surah Al-Mulk (67) — split across rak'ahs
    • Al-Muzzammil 73:1–10 — Allah's call to stand at night
    • Al-Isra 17:78–82 — the Tahajjud verses
    • Ad-Duha (93) and Al-Inshirah (94) — hope and ease
    • Beginners: the last 10 surahs of Juz 'Amma.
    "The best prayer is the one with long standing" (Sahih Muslim 756). Many scholars allow reading from a mushaf in nafl prayer if you haven't memorised much.
  3. Ruku: Subhana Rabbiyal-'Adhim ×3 (or more). Sujood: Subhana Rabbiyal-A'la ×3, then make long dua — "The closest a servant is to his Lord is in sujood, so increase in dua" (Sahih Muslim 482).
  4. Witr (3 rak'ahs): the Prophet ﷺ recited Al-A'la (87) in the 1st, Al-Kafirun (109) in the 2nd and Al-Ikhlas (112) in the 3rd (Sunan Abi Dawud 1423). Recite Dua al-Qunut in the 3rd rak'ah (below).
  5. After Witr: Subhanal-Malikil-Quddus ×3, raising your voice on the third (Sunan Abi Dawud 1430).
After the prayer — your dua time
  1. Sit facing the qiblah, raise your hands. Begin by praising Allah and sending Durood.
  2. Make Istighfar — Sayyidul Istighfar, then Astaghfirullah many times. "And in the hours before dawn they would seek forgiveness" (Qur'an 51:18).
  3. Read your Personal Dua List (Duas tab): Akhirah first, then iman, parents, studies, rizq, health, family, the whole Ummah.
  4. Ask for help leaving your bad habits by name. Ask with certainty, and repeat 3 times.
  5. End with Durood and Rabbana atina fid-dunya hasanah…

Inside the prayer use Arabic duas from the Qur'an and Sunnah. Ask in your own language after salam with raised hands (some scholars also allow your own language in the sujood of nafl prayers).

🤲 Salat al-Hajah — the Prayer of Need
When to pray

My daily plan: every night after Isha, or in the last third of the night together with Tahajjud. If missed, in the morning after sunrise (Duha time).

Also whenever you have a special need: exams, results, admission, job, health, debt, family problems, a bad habit you can't leave. Pray at any time except the forbidden times. Best: in the last third of the night, or before an important event (e.g. the night before or the morning of an exam).

Step by step
  1. Make sincere tawbah first and leave any haram you are in — sins block dua. The Prophet ﷺ mentioned a man whose food, drink and clothing were haram: "How can he be answered?" (Sahih Muslim 1015).
  2. Make wudu carefully and completely.
  3. Niyyah in your heart: 2 rak'ahs of Salat al-Hajah for Allah.
  4. Rak'ah 1: Thana, Fatihah, then any surah. Suggested: Ad-Duha (93) — "Your Lord has not abandoned you".
  5. Rak'ah 2: Fatihah, then Al-Inshirah (94) — "With hardship comes ease" — or Al-Ikhlas.
  6. In sujood, after the tasbih, say the dua of Yunus (AS): La ilaha illa Anta, subhanaka inni kuntu minaz-zalimin.
  7. Finish with Tashahhud, Durood and salam.
  8. After salam: praise Allah (e.g. Alhamdu lillahi Rabbil-'alamin), send Durood, read the Hajah duas below, then ask for your need clearly in your own words. Finish with Durood.
  9. Then do your part — study, apply, see the doctor — and trust Allah. Tie your camel and trust in Allah (Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2517).

No specific surahs are authentically fixed for Salat al-Hajah — the suggestions above are for meaning. Basis: "Whoever performs wudu well, then prays two rak'ahs completing them, Allah gives him what he asks, sooner or later" (Musnad Ahmad 27497); "Seek help through patience and prayer" (Qur'an 2:45).

Etiquette of a dua that gets answered
  1. Begin with praise of Allah and Durood (Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3477).
  2. Face the qiblah, raise your hands, be humble, and be certain Allah will answer (Tirmidhi 3479).
  3. Repeat it 3 times, and call Allah by His Names: Ya Razzaq, Ya Shafi, Ya Fattah, Ya Arhamar-Rahimin.
  4. Don't be impatient: a dua is answered as long as you don't say "I prayed but wasn't answered" (Sahih al-Bukhari 6340). Allah gives it, or saves it for the Akhirah, or protects you from a harm (Musnad Ahmad 11133).
  5. Never ask for something haram, or for cutting family ties.

🧭 Salat al-Istikhara — the Prayer for Guidance
When to pray

My daily plan: every night right after Salat al-Hajah (after Isha or in Tahajjud time), for as long as I have a decision to make — write it in the "My Hajah / Istikhara is for…" box on Today.

Use it before any permissible decision: choosing a course, college or subject; a job or business; marriage; travel; a big purchase. Not for things that are fard (just do them) or haram (just leave them). Do your research and consult wise, trustworthy people first: "and consult them in the matter, then when you have decided, rely on Allah" (Qur'an 3:159).

Pray at any time except the forbidden times. The last third of the night is excellent, because dua is most hopeful then.

Step by step
  1. Make wudu. Be clear in your mind about the one decision you are asking about.
  2. Niyyah in your heart: 2 rak'ahs of Istikhara for Allah.
  3. Rak'ah 1: Fatihah + Al-Kafirun (109). Rak'ah 2: Fatihah + Al-Ikhlas (112). (Recommended by Imam an-Nawawi in al-Adhkar; any surah is valid.)
  4. Complete the prayer with Tashahhud, Durood and salam.
  5. After salam: praise Allah, send Durood, then recite the Istikhara dua (below). Where it says hadhal-amra ("this matter"), name your decision — e.g. "joining this college".
  6. End with Durood.

Basis: Jabir (RA) said the Prophet ﷺ taught them Istikhara in all matters, just as he taught them a surah of the Qur'an — Sahih al-Bukhari 1162. If you can't memorise the dua, read it from your phone after the prayer.

After Istikhara — how to know the answer
  1. You do not need a dream, a feeling or a sign.
  2. Go ahead with what you decided after consulting people. If it is good for you, Allah will make it easy and bless it. If not, He will turn it away from you, and you will see obstacles.
  3. Be content with what happens — the dua itself asks Allah to "make me content with it".
  4. Still unsure? You may repeat Istikhara on the following days.
  5. Some scholars (e.g. an-Nawawi) said the Istikhara dua may also be said after any Sunnah prayer, such as the Sunnah of Dhuhr, with the intention of Istikhara.
Istikhara duas
Don't pray nafl at these times (Sahih Muslim 831): from Fajr until the sun has fully risen · when the sun is at its peak (just before Dhuhr) · after Asr until sunset. You can still make dua at those times — and the last hour after Asr on Friday is a special time for dua (Sunan Abi Dawud 1048). Tahajjud is at night, so it's never affected.

Why prayer helps you quit bad habits

"Indeed, prayer prohibits immorality and wrongdoing." — Qur'an 29:45

"Do not despair of the mercy of Allah. Indeed, Allah forgives all sins." — Qur'an 39:53

If you slip, don't quit the tracker. Make tawbah (sincere repentance), mark the day honestly, and continue. Consistency beats perfection.

How to Pray 2 Rak'ahs (e.g. Fajr)

Rak'ah 1
  1. Takbir: raise hands — Allahu Akbar.
  2. Thana: Subhanaka Allahumma wa bihamdika, wa tabarakasmuka, wa ta'ala jadduka, wa la ilaha ghayruk.
  3. A'udhu billahi minash-shaytanir-rajim. Bismillahir-Rahmanir-Rahim.
  4. Al-Fatihah (every rak'ah), then Amin.
  5. A short surah, e.g. Al-Kawthar: Inna a'taynakal-kawthar. Fasalli li-Rabbika wanhar. Inna shani'aka huwal-abtar.
  6. Ruku: Subhana Rabbiyal-'Adhim ×3.
  7. Rise: Sami'Allahu liman hamidah — Rabbana wa lakal-hamd.
  8. Sujood: Subhana Rabbiyal-A'la ×3.
  9. Sit: Rabbigh-fir li, Rabbigh-fir li.
  10. Second Sujood: Subhana Rabbiyal-A'la ×3.
Rak'ah 2 & ending
  1. Stand with Allahu Akbar. Fatihah + another short surah (e.g. Al-Ikhlas).
  2. Ruku and two Sujood as before.
  3. Tashahhud: At-tahiyyatu lillahi was-salawatu wat-tayyibat. As-salamu 'alayka ayyuhan-Nabiyyu wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh. As-salamu 'alayna wa 'ala 'ibadillahis-salihin. Ash-hadu an la ilaha illallah, wa ash-hadu anna Muhammadan 'abduhu wa rasuluh.
  4. Durood Ibrahim: Allahumma salli 'ala Muhammadin wa 'ala ali Muhammad, kama sallayta 'ala Ibrahima wa 'ala ali Ibrahim, innaka Hamidun Majid. Allahumma barik 'ala Muhammadin wa 'ala ali Muhammad, kama barakta 'ala Ibrahima wa 'ala ali Ibrahim, innaka Hamidun Majid.
  5. Dua: Rabbana atina fid-dunya hasanatan wa fil-akhirati hasanatan wa qina 'adhaban-nar.
  6. Taslim: right then left — As-salamu 'alaykum wa rahmatullah.

For 3 and 4 rak'ah prayers, after Tashahhud in rak'ah 2, stand up for the remaining rak'ahs (Fatihah only), then finish with the full sitting as above. Learn the details from a trusted local scholar.

🆘 Urge SOS

  1. Stop & seek refuge: A'udhu billahi minash-shaytanir-rajim (Qur'an 7:200).
  2. Change your position / leave the place. Put the phone down, go to another room, go outside.
  3. Make wudu. It cools anger and desire and resets your mind.
  4. Say: Ya Muqallibal-qulubi thabbit qalbi 'ala dinik — "O Turner of hearts, keep my heart firm on Your religion."
  5. Remember: "Allah is watching" — and the sweetness of iman for leaving something for His sake.
  6. Pray 2 rak'ahs or read a page of Qur'an. "Prayer prohibits immorality" (29:45).
  7. Call or meet a good friend / family member. Don't stay alone with the urge.

My Student Pledge

For the next 100 days, with Allah's help, I commit as a student to:

  1. Pray all 5 Fard prayers on time, in the Masjid with jama'ah — not at home. Never skip, never delay without reason.
  2. Recite my morning & evening adhkar and daily duas.
  3. Stay away from haram: no cheating in exams, no lying, no backbiting, no haram content, lower my gaze, no alcohol/drugs, no haram earnings.
  4. Study with honesty and effort — knowledge is an amanah (trust).
  5. Respect my parents and make dua for them every day.
  6. Guard my time and phone — health and free time are two blessings most people waste (Bukhari 6412).
  7. Pray Tahajjud at least once a week, and turn to Salat al-Hajah and Istikhara for my needs and decisions.
  8. When I fall, make tawbah and get back up the same day.

"The most beloved deeds to Allah are those done consistently, even if small." — Bukhari 6464

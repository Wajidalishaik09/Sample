Namaz 100 Privacy Policy

App: Namaz 100 — Salah Tracker (com.namaz100.app) · Last updated: 14 November 2026

In short: Namaz 100 does not collect, store or share any of your personal data on any server. Everything you enter stays on your own phone. The app has no account, no ads and no analytics, and it works offline.

1. Data you enter

Your prayer records, checklists, notes, duas, habits, settings and name (optional) are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. We (the developer) never receive this data and cannot see it.

2. Location

If you tap "Use my location", the app asks Android for your approximate location once, to calculate prayer times for your city. The coordinates are saved on your device only and are never sent anywhere. You can instead type your latitude and longitude yourself, or deny the permission; the rest of the app keeps working.

3. Camera (Masjid photo)

For some daily missions, brothers are asked to take a photo inside the Masjid. The app opens your phone's camera app; the photo is shown once on screen so you can confirm it, and is then discarded. Photos are never saved by the app, never uploaded and never shared. Only the time the photo was taken is stored on your device. The app does not request the camera permission itself.

3b. Notifications and alarms

If you turn on prayer reminders, the app schedules notifications on your device for upcoming prayer times. This uses the notification and alarm permissions and does not involve any server.

4. Backups

When you choose "Export backup", the app creates a file and Android lets you pick where to save it (for example your phone storage or a cloud drive you use). The app only writes the file you asked for. "Import backup" reads only the file you pick.

5. In-app purchase

The 100-Day Challenge is unlocked with a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment and your payment details under Google's own privacy policy; the app only receives confirmation from Google Play that the purchase belongs to your Google account, so it can unlock the challenge. We do not receive your card or payment information.

6. Internet, ads and tracking

The app does not send your data over the internet. Its only network use is Google Play's purchase service for the in-app unlock. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it does not share data with third parties.

7. Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Deleting your data

Use "Reset everything" in the app's Pledge tab, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Because nothing is stored on our side, there is nothing else to delete.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new "Last updated" date.

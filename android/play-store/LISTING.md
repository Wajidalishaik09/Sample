## Full description (max 4000)
Build the habit of praying all 5 prayers on time — in 100 days.

Namaz 100 is a calm, beautiful prayer tracker made for students and young Muslims who want to leave bad habits, stay away from haram and make Salah the centre of their day. No account, no ads, no internet — everything stays on your phone.

🕌 FOR BROTHERS AND SISTERS
• Brothers: mark each prayer as Masjid, Home, Late or Missed. Masjid scores highest — "Prayer in congregation is 27 times better" (Bukhari 645).
• Sisters: mark prayers On time, Late or Missed, with an "Excused" option for the days you don't pray, so your streak is never broken.

⏰ PRAYER TIMES & REMINDERS
• Prayer times calculated on your phone for your location (Karachi, MWL, ISNA, Egypt, Umm al-Qura; Hanafi or Shafi'i Asr).
• Live countdown to the next prayer and a reminder before each prayer (at prayer time, or 10–30 minutes before).

🌙 NIGHT ORDER, TAHAJJUD, HAJAH, ISTIKHARA & WITR
• A clear timeline: Isha → Sunnah → Salat al-Hajah → Istikhara → Witr → Tahajjud → Fajr, with tonight's times.
• Step-by-step guides with what to recite in each rak'ah, Dua al-Qunut, the Istikhara dua and more.

📿 DUAS, ADHKAR & TOOLS
• 25+ authentic duas with Arabic, transliteration, meaning and source.
• Your personal dua list — mark duas as answered.
• Tasbih counter, study focus timer, qada (missed prayer) tracker and an "Urge SOS" plan to beat bad habits.

📅 100-DAY JOURNEY
• Daily score, streaks, a 100-day progress map and milestones.
• English and Hijri (Islamic) calendar with Ashura, Arafah, Ayyam al-Beed and other Sunnah fasting days.
• Numbers in English, Arabic and Urdu, with a practice quiz.

✅ AUTHENTIC, NOT VIRAL MYTHS
Learn what the Qur'an and Sahih Hadith really say about each prayer — and why the viral "Fajr for beauty, Dhuhr for money" lists are not authentic.

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No sign-up, no ads, no tracking, no internet permission. Export a backup file whenever you like.

May Allah make it easy for you and accept your prayers. 🤲

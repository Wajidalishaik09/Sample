## Full description (max 4000)
Build the habit of praying all 5 prayers on time — in 100 days.

FREE: prayer times, Qibla compass, live countdown, prayer reminders, the Night Order (Isha → Hajah → Istikhara → Witr), step-by-step guides, Hijri calendar, tasbih and study tools.
100-DAY CHALLENGE (one-time unlock): write WHY you start, set targets, track every prayer and good deed for 100 days, and unlock 25+ authentic duas.

A calm, beautiful prayer tracker for students and young Muslims who want to leave bad habits, avoid haram and make Salah the centre of their day. No account, no ads, works offline — everything stays on your phone.

🕌 FOR BROTHERS AND SISTERS
• Brothers: mark each prayer as Masjid, Home, Late or Missed. Masjid scores highest — "Prayer in congregation is 27 times better" (Bukhari 645).
• Sisters: mark prayers On time, Late or Missed, with an "Excused" option for the days you don't pray, so your streak is never broken.

⏰ PRAYER TIMES & REMINDERS
• Prayer times for your exact location: use GPS or choose your country and state/city (300+ cities in 80+ countries). The right calculation method for your country is chosen automatically (Karachi, Umm al-Qura, ISNA, MWL, Egypt, Dubai, Kuwait, Qatar, Singapore/Malaysia/Indonesia, Diyanet; Hanafi or Shafi'i Asr).
• Live countdown to the next prayer and a reminder before each prayer (at prayer time, or 10–30 minutes before).
• 🧭 Qibla compass: turn your phone until the Kaaba lines up; it vibrates when you face the Qibla. Works offline.
• 💧 Step-by-step Wudu guide with the dua after wudu.

🌙 NIGHT ORDER, TAHAJJUD, HAJAH, ISTIKHARA & WITR
• A clear timeline: Isha → Sunnah → Salat al-Hajah → Istikhara → Witr → Tahajjud → Fajr, with tonight's times.
• Step-by-step guides with what to recite in each rak'ah, Dua al-Qunut, the Istikhara dua and more.

📿 DUAS, ADHKAR & TOOLS
• 25+ authentic duas with Arabic, transliteration, meaning and source.
• Your personal dua list — mark duas as answered.
• Tasbih counter, study focus timer, qada (missed prayer) tracker and an "Urge SOS" plan to beat bad habits.

📅 100-DAY JOURNEY
• Daily score, streaks, a 100-day progress map and milestones.
• English and Hijri (Islamic) calendar with Ashura, Arafah, Ayyam al-Beed and other Sunnah fasting days.
• Numbers in English, Arabic and Urdu, with a practice quiz.

✅ AUTHENTIC, NOT VIRAL MYTHS
Learn what the Qur'an and Sahih Hadith really say about each prayer — not the viral "Fajr for beauty" myths.

🗺️ 100 DAILY MISSIONS, 100 REWARD DUAS
Every day brings a new mission — from "Pray Fajr in the Masjid" to "Istighfar ×100", "A haram-free day" and "Tahajjud tonight" — in 10 stages from Foundation to Finish Strong. Complete it and unlock an authentic reward dua from the Qur'an and Sunnah: read it and say Ameen. Brothers prove Masjid missions with a live photo taken inside the Masjid (camera only — the photo is never saved or uploaded). Every dua you earn is saved in your collection.

🧠 DAILY ISLAMIC QUIZ (free)
3 new questions every day about Salah, duas, the Qur'an and the Seerah — each answer explained with its source.

⭐ LEVELS, XP & 17 BADGES
Every prayer, Sunnah, Tahajjud, mission and Ameen earns XP. Level up from 🌱 Starter to 🏆 100-Day Champion and unlock badges like 🌅 Fajr Warrior, 🌙 Night Worshipper and 📿 Dhikr 1,000.

📤 SHARE YOUR PROGRESS
Post a beautiful progress card on WhatsApp Status or Instagram and invite your friends.

🎯 YOUR WHY & YOUR TARGETS
When you start, you write the reason you are taking the challenge and choose your targets (Masjid prayers per day, streak goal, Tahajjud nights, daily Qur'an, a habit to quit). Your reason is shown every day with live progress on each target. Salat al-Hajah and Istikhara are optional but strongly recommended.

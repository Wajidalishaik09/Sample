# Namaz 100 — Video Ad Plan & Scripts
App: **Namaz 100: Salah Tracker** · Built by **MRS** · Google Play (Android)

---

## 1. The idea in one line
> "How many Fajr did you miss this month? Start your 100-day Namaz challenge — Bismillah."

**Who it is for:** Muslim students and young people (15–30) who want to pray on time, go to the Masjid and leave bad habits.
**Feeling:** calm, hopeful, honest — never shaming, never promising money or beauty (the app itself teaches that those viral claims are not authentic).

## 5. Script C — Story ad (60 seconds) — "Ahmed's 100 days"
1. **(0–8 s)** Ahmed, a college student, scrolls his phone at 2 AM. Alarm at 5 AM — snooze. He wakes at 8, sees the missed Fajr. Text: *"Day 0."*
2. **(8–16 s)** On a bench outside the Masjid after Jumu'ah, a friend shows him Namaz 100. Bismillah screen. He writes his *why*: "I want my parents to be proud — and Allah to be pleased."
3. **(16–30 s)** Montage: Fajr reminder "Leave for the Masjid now" · walking in the dark · 📸 Masjid photo · mission done · reward dua · Ameen · Urge SOS when tempted by his phone at night · Tahajjud once a week.
4. **(30–42 s)** Struggles honestly: one day he marks **Late** — the app says "Don't give up, make tawbah." Day 21 badge 🔥. Day 40 💎. Parents notice.
5. **(42–52 s)** Day 100: celebration screen **"100 days, 100 missions. MashaAllah!"** He shares his card on WhatsApp Status; friends reply "Send me the app!"
6. **(52–60 s)** End card: "Namaz 100 — your 100 days start today. Free on Google Play. Built by MRS."
*(Close with Qur'an 29:45 on screen: "Indeed, prayer prohibits immorality and wrongdoing.")*

## 6. How to shoot it (phone only)
- **App screens:** Android **Screen record** (quick settings) inside the **Namaz 100 Preview** app. Use a clean phone: full battery, no notifications (turn on Do Not Disturb), set the name to "Ahmed".
- **Real-life shots (B-roll):** Masjid outside at dawn, shoes at the Masjid door, wudu water, prayer mats, street lights at Fajr, a student studying.
  - Ask permission before filming inside a Masjid. **Don't film people while they pray**, and don't show faces without permission. Never film sisters without consent.
- **Light:** golden hour (just after sunrise / before Maghrib) looks best.
- **Audio:** many viewers prefer **no music** — use a **vocal-only nasheed** you have the right to use, or natural sounds (birds, footsteps, water) + calm voice-over. Avoid using the Adhan as background music.
- **Editing apps (free):** CapCut, InShot or VN. Add captions, the MRS logo at the end, and the Google Play badge.

## 8. Paid ads (after the app is live)
- **Google Ads → App campaign → App installs**, choose your app, country India (or more), start with **₹200–300 per day** for a week and see the cost per install.
- Upload your 15 s and 30 s videos + 5 short texts + 2 descriptions (Google mixes them).
- Instagram/Facebook: **Boost** your best Reel to "Muslims / Islamic interests", age 16–30, your city or country.
- Measure: Play Console → **Statistics** (installs) and **Order management** (₹199 sales).

## 9. Google Play promo video (store listing)
1. Make the 16:9 version of Script C (or A).
2. Upload to **YouTube** → visibility **Public or Unlisted**, **no ads/monetization**, not "made for kids", embedding allowed.
3. Play Console → **Main store listing → Video** → paste the YouTube link → Save.

---

## 10. Do's and don'ts
- ✅ Honest promises: "build the habit", "track", "reminders", "authentic duas".
- ✅ Show the 🤍 honesty note — it builds trust.
- ❌ Don't promise money, beauty, success or "guaranteed" rewards for praying.
- ❌ Don't show the paid features as free — say "Free app · 100-Day Challenge unlock ₹199" if you mention price.
- ❌ Don't use other apps', channels' or scholars' videos/logos without permission.

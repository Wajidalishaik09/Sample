# Namaz 100 — Promotion Plan (after publishing)
Play link: **https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.namaz100.app**

## Phase 1 — Launch week (free)
1. **Your circle first:** send the link + 30 s video to family, friends, classmates, teachers — ask each to install and share with 3 people.
2. **WhatsApp:** post the video on your Status every day for 7 days; share in masjid, college, hostel, family and community groups (ask admins first).
3. **Masjid poster with QR code:** print an A4 poster ("Start your 100-day Namaz challenge — scan to download"), ask the imam/committee to put it on the notice board. Best after Jumu'ah.
4. **First reviews:** ask your 12 testers and early users to leave an honest ⭐ rating and a short review. Reply to every review politely (Play Console → Ratings and reviews).
5. **Play Store page check:** listing text, screenshots, feature graphic, promo video (YouTube link) all filled.

## Phase 2 — First 30 days
| Channel | What to do | How often |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram Reels** | 30 s ad, the "100 Days Closer" Flow story, short tips ("3 duas for exams", "How to wake up for Fajr") ending with the app | 3–4 per week |
| **YouTube Shorts** | Same vertical videos; Play link in description + pinned comment | 3–4 per week |
| **WhatsApp Channel** | Create "Namaz 100" channel: daily ayah/hadith, quiz question, reminder to keep the streak | Daily |
| **Facebook groups/pages** | Islamic community pages in your city/country | 2 per week |
| **Students** | College MSA/Islamic societies, hostels, coaching centres: offer a group "100-day challenge" | Once, then follow up |
| **Islamic content creators** | Message small creators (5k–50k followers) — ask for an honest review; offer a free unlock code via Play promo codes | 5 messages per week |

**Content ideas that fit the app (no exaggerated claims):**
- "Day 1 vs Day 30 of praying Fajr in the Masjid" (user stories, with permission)
- "The viral Fajr-for-beauty list is a myth — here's what Sahih Hadith says"
- "How to pray Witr / Istikhara / Hajah" (short guide from the app)
- "Find the Qibla anywhere" (screen recording of the compass)
- Users' share cards (repost with permission)

## Phase 3 — Seasonal boosts
- **Ramadan** (biggest moment of the year): start promoting 2 weeks before. "Make this Ramadan the start of your 100 days."
- **Every Friday (Jumu'ah):** reminder posts + poster at the masjid.
- **Exam season:** "Pray on time + study with barakah" — focus on the study timer and exam duas.
- **New Hijri year / 1 Muharram:** "New year, new habit."

## Phase 4 — Small paid ads (only after reviews look good)
- **Google Ads → App campaign → Installs:** ₹200–300 per day for 7 days, India (or your target countries), upload 15 s + 30 s videos and 5 headlines. Keep it if 1 install costs less than about ₹15–25 and purchases follow.
- **Instagram/Facebook boost:** boost the best-performing Reel, audience 16–35, interests "Islam / Quran / Muslim", your country.
- **Play promo codes:** Play Console → **Monetize with Play → Promo codes** — give free unlocks to creators, giveaways ("comment your why — 10 winners").

## Make the Play Store page rank (ASO)
- Words people search: *namaz tracker, salah tracker, prayer tracker, fajr, namaz time, qibla, dua, muslim habit*. They're already in your title/description — keep them.
- Update the app every month (new missions, fixes) with release notes — active apps rank better.
- Ratings matter most: aim for 4.5★+. The in-app share card and good experience bring reviews.

## Track what works (weekly, 10 minutes)
- Play Console → **Statistics**: installs per day, country.
- **Store listing acquisition**: how many visitors install (conversion %).
- **Order management / Financial reports**: ₹199 purchases.
- Keep a simple sheet: date · what you posted · installs that day · purchases.

## Rules to protect your account
- ❌ Never buy fake reviews or installs, never pay people for 5-star ratings — Google bans accounts for this.
- ❌ Don't spam groups; ask admins first.
- ❌ Don't promise worldly results (money, marriage, success) for using the app.
- ✅ Be honest, helpful, consistent. Make intention: the app helps people pray — the reward is with Allah, the income is a means.

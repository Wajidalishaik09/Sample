# "100 Days Closer" — Story ad for Google Flow
**App:** Namaz 100: Salah Tracker · **Built by MRS** · Google Play
**Length:** ~80 seconds (10 scenes × 8 s) · **Format:** vertical 9:16 (also works 16:9)
**Story:** Two close friends — Ahmed and Bilal — feel far from Allah. They find Namaz 100 on Google Play,
accept the 100-day challenge together, and Ahmed's younger sister Ayesha joins from home.
With patience, honesty and the help of Allah, all three complete their 100 days.

## D. Making it in Google Flow — tips
1. **Create the characters first** (Ahmed, Bilal, Ayesha) and reuse them as references in every scene, so faces match.
2. **One scene = one prompt** (~8 s). Generate 2–4 versions and keep the best; use "extend" for longer moments.
3. **Keep Arabic text and app screens OUT of the AI video** — AI often writes wrong Arabic letters and fake app screens.
   Add the **real app screen recordings** (from *Namaz 100 Preview*) and the **end card** later in **CapCut**.
4. **Don't ask the AI to show people performing Salah step by step** — it often gets the positions wrong.
   Show walking to the masjid, wudu, sitting with the Qur'an, making dua, hugging after prayer — respectful and accurate.
5. **Respect & modesty:** the sister is always in modest hijab, filmed with family, no romantic scenes; no depictions of Prophets or angels; don't show a fake Kaaba.
6. **Sound:** record the voice-over yourself (or ask friends/your sister), add a **vocal-only nasheed** you have rights to, plus soft natural sounds (birds, footsteps, water). Avoid music if your audience prefers it.
7. **Disclose AI:** when posting, mention "AI-generated visuals" if the platform asks (Instagram/YouTube have an "AI-made" label).
8. **Export:** 1080×1920 (9:16) for Reels/Shorts/Status; 1920×1080 (16:9) for YouTube and your Google Play store video.
